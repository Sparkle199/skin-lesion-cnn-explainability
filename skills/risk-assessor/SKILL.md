# Risk Assessor

## Procedure
1. For each change, rate impact and likelihood of harm.
2. Identify anything requiring human or ethical sign-off.
3. Escalate flagged items to the student before proceeding.

## Rules
- When uncertain, escalate. Do not absorb risk silently.
